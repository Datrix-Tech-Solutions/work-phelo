## Approval Gate

Before any claim event is activated:

1. The relevant decision rows above must be marked Approved.
2. The payload contract must include every fact needed by the approved policy.
3. Tenant posting rules must exist in Accounting for the selected source event.
4. Reconciliation endpoints must expose source, outbox, inbox and journal status.
5. Tests must prove idempotency and reversal behavior.
